How to Get Your Discord Token

⚡

Easiest Method: Auto-Detect

Just click the "Auto-Detect from Discord" button in settings. It automatically finds your token if Discord is installed!

Or manually get your token
📺 Watch Video Tutorial (2 min)
⚠️ Security Warning: Your token gives full access to your account. NEVER share it with anyone!
1

Open Developer Tools

In Discord (app or web), press:

Ctrl + Shift + I

On Mac: ⌘ + Option + I

2

Go to Network Tab

Click on the Network tab at the top of the developer tools panel.

3

Filter Requests

In the filter box, type /api then press Ctrl + R to reload.

4

Find Your Token

Click any request → Headers tab → scroll to find:

authorization: YOUR_TOKEN_HERE

Copy the entire token value (starts with a letter or number)

💡 Pro Tip: Your token changes when you change your password or enable 2FA. You'll need to get it again if that happens.

How to Setup Spotify App

Setup takes about 3-5 minutes
📺 Watch Video Tutorial
1

Go to Spotify Developer Dashboard

Open your browser and go to:

developer.spotify.com/dashboard

Log in with your Spotify account

2

Create a New App

Click the green "Create app" button

  • App name: LyricsStatus (or anything you like)
  • App description: Lyrics for Discord
  • Website: Leave empty or add any URL
3

⚠️ Add Redirect URI (Important!)

This is the most important step! Add this exact URI:

http://127.0.0.1:67/callback

Click "Add" after pasting, then scroll down and check "Web API"

4

Accept Terms & Create

Check the "I understand" box at the bottom, then click "Save"

5

Copy Your Credentials

In your new app, click "Settings" to find:

  • Client ID Visible on the page
  • Client Secret Click "View client secret" to reveal
✅ You're all set! Paste both credentials into LyricsStatus and click "Authorize Spotify"

🔧 Troubleshooting

  • Can't authorize? Make sure the Redirect URI is exactly http://127.0.0.1:67/callback
  • No songs showing? Make sure Spotify is playing music, not paused
  • Premium required? No! Free accounts work, but playback controls may be limited
